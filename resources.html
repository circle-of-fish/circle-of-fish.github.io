Resources & Links

What we read, and what we read it with

A working bibliography drawn from five years of seminars, and the archives and tools we actually use.

Reading List

Books the Circle keeps returning to

Asia in Global International Relations

  • Amitav Acharya and Barry Buzan. 2019. The Making of Global International Relations: Origins and Evolution of IR at its Centenary. Cambridge University Press.
  • Rotem Kowner and Walter Demel, eds. 2015. Race and Racism in Modern East Asia: Interactions, Nationalism, Gender and Lineage. Brill.
  • Urs Matthias Zachmann, ed. 2017. Asia after Versailles: Asian Perspectives on the Paris Peace Conference and the Interwar Order, 1919–33. Edinburgh University Press.
  • Adom Getachew. 2019. Worldmaking after Empire: The Rise and Fall of Self-Determination. Princeton University Press.
  • Lydia H. Liu. 2006. The Clash of Empires: The Invention of China in Modern World Making. Harvard University Press.

History of International Law

  • Annabel S. Brett. 2011. Changes of State: Nature and the Limits of the City in Early Modern Natural Law. Princeton University Press.
  • C. H. Alexandrowicz, ed. David Armitage and Jennifer Pitts. 2017. The Law of Nations in Global History. Oxford University Press.
  • Lauren Benton, Adam Clulow, and Bain Attwood, eds. 2017. Protection and Empire: A Global History. Cambridge University Press.
  • Saliha Belmessous, ed. 2014. Empire by Treaty: Negotiating European Expansion, 1600–1900. Oxford University Press.

Archives, Databases, and Tools

What we work with

Archives and databases

Where the sources actually are — Korean, Chinese, Japanese, and Western collections.

National Security Archive
George Washington University
Independent archive founded in 1985 that obtains declassified U.S. records through FOIA and publishes them as free briefing books, sourcebooks and a virtual reading room.
The National Archives (UK)
The National Archives, Kew
The UK's official record repository, with the Discovery catalogue and a growing body of digitized records covering a thousand years of British government.
CrossAsia
Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin (FID Asien, DFG)
German national gateway for Asian studies offering full-text search, digitized manuscripts and prints, licensed databases, a newspaper research companion, and an e-publishing repository.
China Biographical Database (中國歷代人物傳記資料庫)
Harvard University, Academia Sinica, Peking University
Freely accessible relational database of biographical and social-network data on several hundred thousand figures in Chinese history, also distributed as SQLite and via API.
Modern History Databases (近代史數位資料庫)
中央研究院 近代史研究所 (Academia Sinica, IMH)
Integrated portal for the Institute of Modern History's collections, including Qing memorial archives, the Chinese Maritime Customs database, grain prices, modern firms, and women's journals.
JACAR (アジア歴史資料センター)
Japan Center for Asian Historical Records / National Archives of Japan
Digitized images and finding aids for modern Japanese records on relations with neighboring Asian countries, drawn from the National Archives, the MOFA Diplomatic Archives, and the NIDS military archives.
Directory of Open Access Journals (DOAJ)
DOAJ (community-supported nonprofit)
Community-curated index of vetted open-access journals worldwide, with freely downloadable metadata and no charge to readers.
Taiwan History Digital Library (臺灣歷史數位圖書館)
National Taiwan University, Center for Digital Humanities
Full-text and metadata search across more than 100,000 documents in three collections: the Danshui-Xinzhu (淡新) archives, Ming-Qing Taiwan administrative records, and old contract documents.
Kanseki Repository (漢籍リポジトリ)
Christian Wittern / Kyoto University
About nine thousand classical Chinese texts in TEI-flavored plain text, catalogued by KR numbers across 經史子集 and distributed through GitHub for local use.
CiNii Research
National Institute of Informatics (NII), Japan
NII's aggregated search across Japanese scholarly articles, books, dissertations, datasets, research projects and roughly 300,000 researcher profiles.
Internet Archive Scholar
Internet Archive
Full-text search across roughly 86 million academic papers preserved by the Internet Archive, including material from defunct and small-press journals.
Scripta Sinica (漢籍全文資料庫)
中央研究院 歷史語言研究所 (Academia Sinica, IHP)
Full-text database of over 1,582 classical Chinese titles and 879 million characters, weighted toward historical works, with free and licensed access tiers.
Foreign Relations of the United States (FRUS)
Office of the Historian, U.S. Department of State
The official documentary record of U.S. foreign policy from the Lincoln administration (1861) through Clinton (1993–2000), browsable and searchable in full text online.
Ming Qing Women's Writings (明清婦女著作)
中央研究院 近代史研究所 (originally McGill University Library)
Bilingual database of poetry and prose by Ming and Qing women, migrated from McGill to Academia Sinica and updated through 2025.
內閣大庫檔案 (Grand Secretariat Archives)
中央研究院 歷史語言研究所
Academia Sinica's Ming-Qing archives workshop and searchable database of roughly 310,000 Grand Secretariat documents spanning the Ming through the early Republic.
SHIPS 史料編纂所データベース
東京大学史料編纂所 (Historiographical Institute, University of Tokyo)
Portal cross-searching the Institute's databases: Dai Nihon shiryō, full-text ancient and medieval documents, rubbings and prints, name indexes, and the MOJIZO cursive-script reader.
国書データベース (Kokusho Database)
国文学研究資料館 (National Institute of Japanese Literature)
Free union catalogue of pre-Meiji Japanese books integrating the Kokusho sōmokuroku and the New Comprehensive Database of Japanese Classics, with high-resolution images targeting 300,000 titles.
国立公文書館デジタルアーカイブ (National Archives of Japan Digital Archive)
国立公文書館
Free, registration-free access to digitized Japanese public documents, maps, and classical Japanese and Chinese texts, with cross-search into partner prefectural archives.
国立国会図書館デジタルコレクション (NDL Digital Collections)
国立国会図書館 (National Diet Library)
The National Diet Library's mass-digitized books, periodicals, and rare materials, a large share of them out of copyright and readable worldwide.
教育部異體字字典 (Dictionary of Chinese Character Variants)
國家教育研究院 / 教育部, Taiwan
Official seventh-edition reference for variant Chinese character forms, searchable by radical, stroke count, structure, Zhuyin, Pinyin, or character code.
早稲田大学古典籍総合データベース (Waseda Kotenseki Database)
Waseda University Library
Openly published color images and bibliographic data for Waseda's classical Japanese and Chinese book collection, including two National Treasures.
清代宮中檔奏摺及軍機處檔摺件
國立故宮博物院 (National Palace Museum, Taipei)
Free metadata and document images for 158,614 palace memorials and 190,889 Grand Council documents, part of a system of over 400,000 Qing archival items.
국가기록원 (National Archives of Korea)
행정안전부
Korea's central government archive, with over six million records online plus 850,000+ colonial-period documents, gazettes, cabinet minutes, and independence-movement trial records.
승정원일기 (Diaries of the Royal Secretariat)
국사편찬위원회
The Royal Secretariat diaries covering twelve reigns from Injo (1623) to Sunjong (1907), with indexes of persons, places, books, and medical terms.
외교사료관 (Diplomatic Archives of Korea)
외교부 (Ministry of Foreign Affairs, ROK)
The ROK foreign ministry's archive of declassified diplomatic documents, searchable by theme, country, and year of release, with a reading room and an open-documents portal.
규장각 원문검색서비스 (Kyujanggak Original Text Search)
규장각한국학연구원, 서울대학교
Catalogue, bibliographic descriptions,解題, and full original-text images for the Kyujanggak collection, including uigwe, old maps, cadastral records, and diplomatic documents.
장서각 (Jangseogak Digital Archive)
한국학중앙연구원 (Academy of Korean Studies)
Online access to the Jangseogak royal archive's manuscripts, rare books, edicts and correspondence, with high-resolution images and scholarly descriptions.
전자사료관 (NIKH Electronic Archive)
국사편찬위원회
Digitized Korea-related records collected from foreign repositories and private holders in the U.S., Japan, China, Taiwan, Russia, the UK, France, Germany and a dozen more countries.
조선왕조실록 (Veritable Records of the Joseon Dynasty)
국사편찬위원회
Searchable full text of the Sillok from Taejo (1392) to Sunjong (1910), with the classical Chinese original and modern Korean translation side by side.
한국역대인물 종합정보시스템 (Korean Historical Figures Information System)
한국학중앙연구원
Prosopographical database of historical Korean figures built on civil-service examination rosters, genealogies, and biographical sources.
한국고전종합DB (Korean Classics Database)
Institute for the Translation of Korean Classics (한국고전번역원)
Comprehensive database of classical Korean and Sinitic texts with over 260 translated works and 1,259 authors' collected writings, offering original images, collated and punctuated versions, annotation, and machine translation of classical Chinese.
한국민족문화대백과사전 (Encyclopedia of Korean Culture)
한국학중앙연구원
Open scholarly encyclopedia of Korean history, thought, religion and culture with over 100,000 signed entries and an OpenAPI.
한국사데이터베이스 (Korean History Database)
National Institute of Korean History (국사편찬위원회)
Six-part database spanning ancient, Goryeo, Joseon, modern, and contemporary Korean history, including the Joseon royal annals, administrative records, newspapers, photographs, and GIS materials, with thesaurus and multilingual search.
한국학자료센터 (Korean Studies Materials Center)
한국학중앙연구원
Roughly one million historical documents — royal protocols, household registers, land deeds, academy records — gathered through regional centers across Korea and overseas.

Tools, maps, and blogs

David Rumsey Map Collection
Stanford University Libraries
Over 150,000 freely viewable historical maps and cartographic images from the sixteenth century onward, with georeferencing and MapRank search tools.
COMPARATIVUS
Leiden University / MARKUS project
Text-reuse tool that compares two or more texts, reports similarity scores, and highlights matching passages with adjustable k-size and match thresholds.
MIT Visualizing Cultures
Massachusetts Institute of Technology
Image-driven scholarly units on modern Japan and late imperial China, treating visual sources on war, modernization, consumerism and images of self and other.
World Historical Gazetteer
University of Pittsburgh
Platform linking historical place records across time and language, indexing 47 million places and 67 million toponyms with phonetic search across scripts.
Gephi
Gephi Consortium (nonprofit)
Open-source visual network analysis software handling graphs from ten to ten million nodes, with community detection, centrality measures and layout algorithms.
MARKUS
Leiden University / Chinese Empires in Comparative Perspective
Free browser-based platform that auto-tags personal names, place names, dates, offices and Buddhist terms in classical Chinese texts and exports to GIS and visualization tools.
Connected Sociologies Curriculum Project
The Sociological Review Foundation
Open-access curriculum of nine modules on colonialism, migration, policing, inequality, and climate, with video lectures by academic experts and downloadable lesson plans under a Creative Commons licence.
QGIS
QGIS.org / OSGeo
Free and open-source desktop GIS for Windows, Mac and Linux, covering map creation, layer editing, spatial analysis and web publishing under GPLv2+.
Recogito
Pelagios Network (Mellon-funded)
Free open-source web platform for semantic annotation of texts and images — marking named entities, working collaboratively, and exporting annotations for other tools.
Global Social Theory
Project associated with Gurminder K. Bhambra
Free reference resource organizing social theory in global perspective through entries on concepts, thinkers, and topics, plus a dedicated section on decolonising the university, all under Creative Commons licence.
Transkribus
READ-COOP SCE
AI platform for recognizing handwritten and printed historical text in over 100 languages, with layout detection, tagging and publishing, offering 50 free credits monthly.
Tropy
Roy Rosenzweig Center for History and New Media, C²DH, Digital Scholar
Free open-source desktop application for organizing, describing, transcribing and annotating photographs of archival documents, exporting to JSON-LD, CSV and Omeka S.
Sinica Digital Atlas
Historical GIS of China from Academia Sinica.
Chinese Pinyin Converter
Hanzi to pinyin with tone marks.
Chinese–Western Calendar Converter
Academia Sinica
Converts imperial Chinese reign dates to Julian and Gregorian.
Korean Romanization Converter
Pusan National University
Transliterates Hangul into the Revised Romanization.
Lunar–Solar Calendar Converter
Korea Astronomy and Space Science Institute
Converts Korean lunar dates to the Gregorian calendar.
Reading the China Dream
Translations of and commentary on contemporary Chinese intellectual life.
Northeast Asia HistoryNet
Northeast Asian History Foundation's source collections on Korea and its neighbours.
Qing Maps
Digitized Qing cartography with georeferencing.
Citation Style for Chinese, Japanese and Korean Sources
Yale University Library
Chicago Manual examples for CJK sources.
Digital Resources for Sinology Studies in Taiwan
Academia Sinica's gateway to Taiwanese digital sinology collections.
Chinese–English Dictionary of Ming Government Official Titles
Open-access reference for Ming office names.
Warp, Weft, and Way
Group blog on Chinese and comparative philosophy.
