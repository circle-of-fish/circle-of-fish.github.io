Members

Nine scholars, one ontological worry

We study disparate subjects — empire and international law, naval power and status, emotion and memory, human rights and gender, the Cold War making of Korean IR. What we share is the suspicion that the discipline's standard inventory of what exists does not describe the worlds we work on.
