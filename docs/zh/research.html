研究

取向、方法与两个研究模块

把河豚会联结起来的，不是共同的研究对象，而是共同的怀疑：学科视为理所当然的那些存在者——在无政府状态下依循单一生存逻辑追求安全的主权国家——不过是切分「国际」的一种历史上特定的方式，却被误认为唯一的方式。以下的一切，都源于认真对待这一怀疑。

理论取向

五项承诺

这并非河豚会强制推行的教条，而是我们已发表的研究反复采取的做法——写得足够明确，好让读者据此追问我们。

01

Orders are ontologically complex, not merely culturally diverse

We do not treat the Qing, the Ming–Chosŏn ritual order, or today's great-power rivalry as one reality described in several idioms. When Chosŏn envoys met the Panchen Lama at Qianlong's birthday, the Confucian party experienced a subhuman creature and the Lama experienced ignorant lay beings — different lived worlds inside a single order, not different opinions about it (Choi and Kwon, EJIR 2025). That premise, worked out through the ontological turn and read back from Pak Chiwŏn's own critique of the Qing court's appropriation of peripheral ontologies, also drives our accounts of U.S.–China rivalry as an encounter of ontological others and of a divided peninsula as a pre-individual field rather than two finished states.

02

Interpolity order, not the international system

Our unit of analysis is the interpolity order — a configuration of polities held together by ritual, virtue, status and recognition rather than by mutual sovereign recognition. 'Chinese' hegemony was a reciprocal rule of virtue, an aretocracy, in which authority belonged to those who demonstrated civil excellence; sadae contains the seed of a monadological theory in which states are non-sovereign monads attuned to the coherence of the whole; the Spanish bid for Morocco in 1940 makes sense only against the organizing principle of an inter-imperial Europe from which Spain had been expelled. Sovereignty is one historical form among several, and we trace its arrival rather than assume it.

03

Order is made by sociocultural forces, not by states alone

The transnational shi literati of China, Korea and Vietnam steered the foreign policy of what Choi calls symbiotic states and underwrote the normative basis of tributary relations; Ming and Chosŏn literati built envoy-poetry exchange into the medium of hegemonic co-construction. The same commitment runs through our contemporary work: rebel groups and the ICRC negotiating child-rights legitimacy, Amnesty's network form, colonial Korean magazines, K-pop fandom, Mexican women's everyday cooperation with cartels. Agency in world politics is distributed well below and across the state.

04

Hierarchy is racialized, gendered, and worn on the body

Our second research module treats the standard of civilization as something its subjects helped constitute. Colonized Korean women defending bobbed hair as hygienic, efficient and emancipatory were arguing about modernity under three stacked hierarchies at once (Kwon, EJIR 2025); Japan traded naval tonnage for civilizational standing even after the racial equality proposal was rejected and the 1924 Exclusion Act passed (Oh 2025); Korean elites turned Eurocentrism itself into a technique of self-strengthening. Gendered protection in humanitarian law, and the media frames that convert misogyny into sarcasm, belong to the same argument.

05

Pluralization by complexification, not by national schools

We reject the idea that a non-Western IR means a Chinese, Korean or Japanese national school. Chineseness has always been a contested, aspirational, transcultural ethos, so a durable Chinese IR must complexify its own tradition internally and others externally (Choi, RIS 2023); Korean IR was not an Americanized import but a bricolage in which Lee Yong-hee and his contemporaries reassembled colonial, Japanese and American materials into a knowledge order fitted to Korea's predicament (Ok). Relationalism, on the same reading, is not a position one adopts but continuous labour against the dualist grain of scholarly writing (Jackson and Heo 2022).

方法论取向

五项承诺

存在论层面的主张，其分量取决于支撑它的那份读解。因此河豚会的方法论取向，大体上是关于史料的取向：读哪些，用哪种语言，怎么读。

01

以意逆志 (iuiyeokji): reading intention against intention

Our 2023 methods workshop, held over five days at the East Asia Institute, built an interpretive approach to East Asian diplomatic sources out of a classical Confucian hermeneutic principle — meeting the text's will with one's own rather than extracting variables from it. In practice this is joined to Ricoeur's distanciation and Strathern's ethnographic moment: we let a source deconstruct the frame we brought to it, then rebuild the frame, which is why an envoy's travelogue can yield an argument about ontological complexity rather than an illustration of tribute.

02

Multilingual archival work across incommensurable record sets

We read literary Chinese, Korean, Japanese, Spanish and Tibetan records against one another instead of privileging whichever archive is best catalogued. Members work with Chosŏn envoy records and Tibetan accounts of the same encounter, colonial-Korean periodicals, Iberian scholastic natural law as it reached sixteenth-century Japan, Cold War Korean and American files, Philippine and Nepali field interviews, and Admiralty and Foreign Ministry papers. Training is deliberate: Jangseogak classical Chinese, Qing document seminars, the Institute for the Translation of Korean Classics, Filipino, Tibetan and Mongolian civilization.

03

Conceptual history and the future-passed

Following Koselleck, whom we read together, we treat concepts as carrying sedimented futures. The 1636 anti-accommodation debate is approached as a 'future-passed' rather than a specimen of the tributary system; geopolitics in early Cold War Korea is tracked as it converts 숙명 (fate) into 운명 (destiny); internationalism, dependency, and 'Korean Studies' are followed as they are translated, localized and institutionalized. Lydia Liu's work on translingual practice and our seminars on Getachew, Zarakol and Alexandrowicz set the terms.

04

Historical process tracing and comparative case work

We build mid-range theory from paired and sequenced cases: two Anglo-French dyads and the U.S.–Japan dyad for naval power projection; the Ming–Qing transition, the Westphalian transition and Détente for status ascent; four phases from violation through blacklisting to compliance for rebel commitments, combining large-N, QCA and congruence tests. Several members trained at IQMR, and process tracing, concept analysis and large-N qualitative analysis are the shared vocabulary that lets historical and contemporary projects speak to each other.

05

Interpretive social science and knowing-from-within

We hold that scholarly, expert and practitioner knowledge alike are produced relationally, from inside a community of practice, rather than by detached representation (Jackson and Heo, 'Ways of Knowing', 2025). Methodologically this licenses interviews and fieldwork alongside archives — thirty-seven interviews in Jalisco, repeated fieldwork in Mindanao and Nepal, survey experiments where the question is how a public reads a victim — and it obliges us to state our own position in the encounter rather than bracket it away.

研究模块

两个共同研究计划

模块一

历史上东亚国际秩序中的权力、制度与观念

本模块致力于系统地理论化历史上东亚国际秩序中的物质、观念与制度结构及其变迁，时段自近世延伸至二十世纪初。与关于近世和近代欧洲的成熟研究相比，对东亚物质、观念与空间结构的研究仍显粗略，理论化程度不足。

既有文献大多集中于把东亚独特的组织原则与欧洲主权国家体系及均势加以比较。本模块则以近年迅速积累的东亚国际秩序史研究为基础，考察这些结构的运作要素，以及它们在关键历史节点上的延续与转变。

研究领域
  • 东亚国际法与国际思想的思想史与概念史
  • 东亚及其以外相互连结的海洋势力与海洋秩序
  • 东亚的权力、扩散与场域（topos）
  • 东亚国际秩序中的地位与制度性等级
模块二

历史上东亚国际政治中的种族与性别

本模块探讨历史上东亚国际政治中的种族与性别不平等——不是作为权力与制度研究的补论，而是作为对那种权力如何被构成、那种等级如何变得可读的说明。

研究领域
  • 关于种族与性别的去殖民与复数世界（pluriversal）路径
  • 重估日本帝国主义与战时占领时期的种族化、性别化暴力
  • 性别规范与女性在国际社会形成中的作用
  • 在西方种族意识形态的脉络中，日本殖民统治如何在东亚强加种族等级
  • 国际制度与国际法中的欧洲中心主义种族偏见
